# Metric Sovereignty

Social choice, AI directives, and capitalist democracy. A living draft by Ian T. Helfrich.

Read it at [ihelfrich.github.io/metric-sovereignty](https://ihelfrich.github.io/metric-sovereignty/).

Arrow's theorem says no rule can turn unrestricted individual rankings into a coherent collective ranking while satisfying four reasonable conditions. AI-assisted governance does not repeal this; it changes where the impossibility lands. When a learned political geometry selects policies by minimizing distance in a latent space, the metric itself becomes a site of political authority. The paper names that authority, traces how concentrated economic power already acts through it, and works out what Arrow's result implies for anyone proposing to govern with a loss function.
